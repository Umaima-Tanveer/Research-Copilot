# Research Copilot AI

## Overview

Research Copilot AI is a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) multi-agent framework for automated scientific literature analysis and academic manuscript generation. The framework transforms collections of research papers into structured knowledge and orchestrates multiple specialized AI agents to generate research maps, literature reviews, research gap analyses, research contributions, research questions, review papers, and complete research papers.

Unlike traditional single-agent systems, Research Copilot AI follows a modular multi-agent architecture where each agent performs a specialized research task and passes structured outputs to downstream agents. Human validation can be incorporated throughout the workflow to improve reliability, transparency, and academic quality.

## Workflow Description

### Step 1 – Knowledge Extraction

The framework begins by processing scientific research papers in PDF format. Important information is extracted from every paper, including:

- Metadata
- Abstract
- Keywords
- Methodology
- Models
- Datasets
- Experimental Setup
- Evaluation Metrics
- Results
- Contributions
- Limitations
- Future Work

The extracted information is organized into a Structured Knowledge Base (SKB), which acts as the central repository for all downstream agents.

---

### Step 2 – Research Map Agent

The Research Map Agent analyzes the Structured Knowledge Base to identify research domains, methodologies, datasets, models, evaluation metrics, and relationships among the collected papers. It also generates visual representations of the research landscape.

Outputs

- Research Map (JSON)
- Research Map Visualization

---

### Step 3 – Literature Review Agent

The Literature Review Agent synthesizes the information stored in the Structured Knowledge Base and produces a comprehensive literature review by grouping related works, comparing methodologies, summarizing findings, and identifying research trends.

Outputs

- Literature Review (JSON)
- Literature Review (.csv)

---

### Step 4 – Research Gap Agent

The Research Gap Agent analyzes the Structured Knowledge Base to identify limitations, unresolved challenges, inconsistencies, and unexplored research opportunities across the surveyed literature.

Outputs

- Research Gaps
- Open Challenges
- Future Research Directions

---

### Step 5 – Contribution Agent

The Contribution Agent combines the outputs generated by the Literature Review Agent and the Research Gap Agent to propose novel research contributions that address the identified research gaps.

Inputs

- Literature Review
- Research Gap

Outputs

- Proposed Contributions
- Novel Research Ideas
- Expected Research Impact

---

### Step 6 – Research Question Agent

The Research Question Agent formulates clear research objectives and questions by utilizing the outputs of the Contribution Agent together with the identified Research Gaps.

Inputs

- Contribution
- Research Gap

Outputs

- Research Questions
- Research Objectives
- Research Hypotheses

---

### Step 7 – Review Paper Agent

The Review Paper Agent directly utilizes the Structured Knowledge Base to automatically generate a complete review paper.

Outputs

- Review Paper (JSON)
- Review Paper (LaTeX)
- Review Paper (PDF)

---

### Step 8 – Research Paper Agent

The Research Paper Agent receives the generated Review Paper together with user-provided research information such as methodology, datasets, experiments, and results to generate a complete research manuscript.

Outputs

- Research Paper (JSON)
- Research Paper (LaTeX)
- Research Paper (PDF)

---

## Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

Research Copilot AI integrates Human-in-the-Loop validation throughout the workflow. Researchers can inspect, edit, approve, or refine intermediate outputs before they are forwarded to downstream agents, ensuring greater transparency, reliability, and academic quality.
